1. Who provides the service

Kitom is developed and provided by David Zarzavilla Borrego, a private individual resident in Spain ("Kitom", "we"). Contact: dzarzavillaborrego@gmail.com.

2. What Kitom is

Kitom is an app that supports the emotional and behavioural wellbeing of dogs and cats. It lets you:

  • create your pets' profiles;
  • log how they are each day and see their history, streaks and achievements;
  • keep an agenda with reminders;
  • ask for AI guidance based on a photo and the signs you notice;
  • share a pet with other people (co-owners).

3. It is not veterinary care

Nothing Kitom shows is a medical assessment, a prescription or professional veterinary advice. Kitom doesn't examine your pet and can't know everything that is happening to them. The information helps you observe better and decide when to see a vet. You are responsible for your pet's health and care, and whenever in doubt you should see a vet. Don't delay or avoid veterinary care because of something you saw in Kitom.

4. AI guidance

  • The guidance is prepared by an artificial intelligence model from the photo, signs and data you provide. It is informative and indicative only: it may be incomplete or wrong.
  • It shows possible causes, care at home and warning signs that call for a vet. It doesn't confirm any illness.
  • Each account can request 3 guidance checks per month. The counter restarts on the 1st of each month. We may change this limit with prior notice.
  • To prepare it we send check data to our AI provider, as explained in the privacy policy.
  • If a person appears in the photo, take it again. Only send photos you have the right to use.
