Good care is a sum of days

Kitom keeps you company in your dog's or cat's day to day: log how they are in under a minute, understand their changes and know when a vet is needed.

  • Daily log of mood, energy, appetite, sleep and behaviour, even offline.
  • History by week and month, with streaks and achievements.
  • Agenda with notifications for vaccines, medication and appointments.
  • AI guidance from a photo and what you notice: possible causes, care at home and warning signs.
  • Co-owners: share your pet with whoever looks after them with you.

Kitom does not replace a vet. Its guidance is for information only. In an emergency, go to a vet.
