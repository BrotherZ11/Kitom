Privacy policy

Effective from 30 October 2026

Kitom is an app that keeps you company in your dog's or cat's wellbeing. Here we explain, with no small print, what data we process, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and how you stay in control. The Spanish version is the reference version.

In short

  • We only process what the app needs to work: your account and what you log about your pets.
  • To prepare AI guidance we send Google the data of that check, without your name or email. Google does not use it to train its models.
  • We don't sell your data, and there are no ads or cross-app tracking.
  • You can download your data and delete your account from the app itself.

3. AI guidance

When you ask for guidance, Kitom sends Google (the Gemini API, through Google AI Studio) only what is needed to prepare it:

  • the photo, if you add one;
  • the signs you choose;
  • pet data: species, breed, age, weight, sex, whether neutered, known health conditions and allergies;
  • their latest daily logs, including notes.

We don't send your name, your email or your pet's name. Please don't write personal data in the notes.

We use the paid Gemini API service. In that service Google acts as a processor: it processes the content only to return the response to us, does not use it to train or improve its models and only keeps it for a limited time to detect abuse, under its terms. Kitom doesn't use your photos or checks to train models or for any other purpose either.

The photo, the signs and the guidance are stored privately with the check, so that you and the people who share the pet with you can see it again. The guidance is for information only: it is not a medical assessment and does not replace a vet. You make the decisions; no decision with legal effects is taken automatically.

7. Your rights

You can exercise these rights over your data at any time:

  • Access and portability: in the app, under Profile › Your account and settings › Download my data, you get a JSON file with your data.
  • Rectification: you can correct your profile, your pets and your logs in the app.
  • Erasure: in the app, under Profile › Your account and settings › Delete account. If you no longer have the app, follow these instructions.
  • Objection and restriction: you can object to processing based on legitimate interest or ask us to restrict the use of your data.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off "Tips and news by email" under Privacy. Withdrawing it doesn't affect what was done before.

For anything else, write to dzarzavillaborrego@gmail.com from your account email. We will reply within one month at most (extendable by two more months for complex cases, letting you know). It is free.

If you think we haven't handled your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, www.aepd.es) or the authority of your EU country of residence. We'd appreciate it if you wrote to us first so we can try to sort it out.

8. Data on your phone and permissions

  • Session: stored in the phone's secure storage.
  • Offline mode: the app keeps a copy of your pets, your logs and the breed catalogue on the phone so you can use it without a connection. When you sign out, anything not uploaded yet stays on the phone so it isn't lost; if everything is uploaded, the copy is deleted from the phone. Everything is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in the system settings.
  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled on your own phone; we don't use notifications sent from a server.
  • Permissions: camera and photos (only when you choose a photo), notifications and, on Android, exact alarms (so notifications arrive on time). You can revoke them in your phone settings.

9. Cookies, ads and tracking

This website uses no cookies, analytics or third-party resources. The app shows no ads, doesn't track you across apps or websites and doesn't share data for advertising.

10. Minimum age

Kitom is for people aged 16 or over. We don't knowingly process data of anyone younger; if you think a minor has given us their data, write to us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Communications are encrypted (HTTPS). Each person can only see their own data and that of pets shared with them: access rules are enforced on the server. Photos are stored in a private space and only shown through temporary links. If a security breach affected your data, we would notify the authority and, where appropriate, you.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version here with its date. If the change is significant, we will let you know in the app or by email before it applies.
